# Noviat

We put Odoo in place for companies and for Belgian public administrations,
and we keep it running afterwards. Partner in Belgium since 2009, implementing
since Odoo version 5.

Accounting, logistics and manufacturing, made-to-measure products, project and
site-based business, banking and electronic invoicing, and our own platform for
local government. Most of that does not live on GitHub. What is here is the part
we open sourced.

## Money in and out of Odoo, without anyone retyping it

Bank statements that arrive on their own, payment files that go out, and
reconciliation that stops being a job someone does by hand every Monday.

Across much of Europe that runs on a banking standard called EBICS, and the
module connecting Odoo to it is ours. We wrote it, we maintain it, and we have
kept it working on every version of Odoo since version 8. It is used with banks
in Belgium, France, Germany, Austria and Switzerland.

## What you will find here

**[account_ebics](https://github.com/Noviat/account_ebics)** is that module,
with the pieces around it: scheduled imports, batch payments, and payment
orders.

**[odoo-text-elements](https://github.com/Noviat/odoo-text-elements)** adds
personalised text blocks to quotations, invoices and purchase orders.

## The rest of what we do

Setting this up, and the Odoo implementation around it, is what we do for a
living.

- **[Finance and accounting](https://www.noviat.com/finance).** Statutory books
  country by country, and the bank connections behind them. We keep Belgian,
  French and German accounts in Odoo, and adding a country is a known exercise
  for us.
- **[Logistics and manufacturing](https://www.noviat.com/logistics-manufacturing).**
  Stock that matches the shelf, and production that receives what was sold.
- **[Made-to-measure products](https://www.noviat.com/make-to-order).**
  Configurators, production that starts at the order rather than the forecast,
  margins tracked per job.
- **[Project and site-based business](https://www.noviat.com/project-business).**
  Hours, materials and margin recorded against the job, visible while it is
  still running.
- **[Ordo](https://www.noviat.com/ordo).** Our own platform, built on Odoo for
  Belgian municipalities, provinces and regions. It manages public assets and
  the work carried out on them: what you own, what you maintain, what you are
  required to inspect, and what all of it costs.
- **[Electronic invoicing](https://www.noviat.com/electronic-invoicing)** and
  [the five ways to connect a bank to Odoo](https://www.noviat.com/bank-connectivity),
  compared side by side.
